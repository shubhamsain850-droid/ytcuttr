How to Cut & Download a YouTube Video from a Specific Time (2026 Guide)

Published by YTCuttr Team • 4 min read

Have you ever needed a 20-second joke, an educational explanation, or a sports play from an hour-long YouTube video, only to realize that downloading the entire 2GB video file just to trim 20 seconds is a complete waste of time, data, and storage?

In this guide, we’ll show you the fastest way to cut and download only the exact segment of any YouTube video or live stream in 1080p HD on iPhone, Android, or PC without installing heavy software.

The 4-Step Method to Cut Any YouTube Video

  1. Copy the YouTube Link: Open the YouTube app or website. Find the video, live stream recording, or Short you want to clip. Click Share and select Copy link.
  2. Open YTCuttr.com: Paste your copied link into the URL input box at YTCuttr.com and hit Get Video. The interactive preview player and trimming timeline will load immediately.
  3. Set Start and End Timestamps: Use the dual sliders or enter the exact timestamps in HH:MM:SS format (e.g. 00:01:15 to 00:01:45). You can also click the +1s / -1s micro-nudge buttons or click Set to Player Time while watching the preview.
  4. Choose Resolution & Download: Select your desired video quality (1080p Full HD, 720p, or Audio Only) and hit Download Clip. YTCuttr slices the segment directly in the cloud and saves the clean MP4 file to your device.
💡 Pro-Tip for Seamless Cuts: When trimming speech or dialogue, always set your start timestamp approximately 0.2 to 0.3 seconds before the speaker utters the first syllable. This prevents the beginning of the word from being clipped off. Likewise, add a fraction of a second after the sentence finishes to let natural room echo fade out.

How to Save Clips on Mobile Devices

On iPhone & iPad (iOS)

Open Safari, navigate to YTCuttr, and download your clip. Safari will prompt you to download the file. Tap the download icon in Safari's address bar, open the file in the Files app, tap the Share icon, and choose Save Video to move it directly to your Photos camera roll.

On Android

Open Chrome, paste your link, and download. The MP4 clip will automatically save directly into your device's Downloads folder and will immediately appear in Google Photos or your Gallery app.
