How to Cut & Download a YouTube Video from a Specific Time (2026 Guide)

Published by YTCuttr Team • Updated November 26, 2026 • 7 min read
Quick Answer (3 Ways Depending on Your Goal)
  • Need an MP4/MP3 file of just one scene: Paste the URL into YTCuttr.com, set Start and End timestamps (up to 5 minutes per cut), and download in 1080p or 4K.
  • Just want a link that plays a start/end window: Use the YouTube Timestamp Link Generator without downloading any file.
  • Fixing a video already uploaded to your channel: Use YouTube Studio → Editor → Trim & cut to trim ends or middle errors without losing views.

Have you ever needed a 25-second explanation, a podcast quote, or an educational demonstration buried inside a three-hour YouTube video? Downloading an entire 2GB video file just to trim half a minute wastes bandwidth, storage, and editing time.

With YouTube having retired its viewer-created Clips button in April 2026 in favor of simple Share at Timestamp links, knowing how to isolate and save a specific video segment cleanly on iPhone, Android, or PC is more useful than ever. Here is the complete 2026 workflow.

Method 1: Cut & Download a Specific Timestamp Segment (Without Downloading the Full Video)

  1. Copy the YouTube URL: Open the YouTube video, Short, or archived live stream. Click Share → Copy link (or copy the browser address bar URL).
  2. Load It in YTCuttr: Open YTCuttr.com (or the 4K YouTube Cutter for 2160p footage), paste the link, and click Get Video. The interactive preview player and timeline load immediately.
  3. Set Your Start and End Timestamps: Use the dual timeline sliders, type exact HH:MM:SS timestamps (for example, 00:14:20 to 00:15:05), or click Set to Player Time while watching. Fine-tune using the -1s / +1s nudge buttons.
  4. Pick Quality & Download: Choose 1080p Full HD, 720p, 4K (when available), or Audio Only, then click Download Clip. YTCuttr slices only that window in the cloud (up to 5 minutes per cut) and delivers a clean, watermark-free file.
💡 Pro-Tip for Clean Dialogue Cuts: When clipping speech or interviews, set your Start timestamp about 0.2 to 0.3 seconds before the first word begins, and add 0.2 seconds after the sentence finishes. That buffer prevents clipped first syllables and awkward abrupt cutoffs.

Method 2: Save Your Trimmed Clip on iPhone, Android & Desktop

On iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

Safari downloads MP4 files into the Apple Files app rather than directly into Photos:

  1. Cut and download your clip in Safari on ytcuttr.com, and tap Download when prompted.
  2. Tap the blue downward arrow in Safari’s address bar → Downloads → tap the MP4 file.
  3. Tap the Share icon in the bottom-left corner and choose Save Video. It immediately moves to your Camera Roll.

On Android (Chrome)

Download the clip in Chrome and it saves directly to your Downloads folder, appearing automatically inside Google Photos, Samsung Gallery, or your editing app (CapCut, VN, Premiere Rush).

On Windows & Mac

Clips save straight to your browser’s Downloads folder as standard H.264/AAC MP4 files compatible with QuickTime, VLC, Windows Media Player, PowerPoint, Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve.

Common Timestamp Cutting Mistakes to Avoid

  • Typing MM:SS into an HH:MM:SS field incorrectly: Entering 01:30 when a field expects hours can either misread minutes or require 00:01:30. Double-check the preview duration readout before hitting Download.
  • Trying to download a 15-minute segment in one go: YTCuttr caps individual clips at 5 minutes to prevent timeout errors and server congestion. For a 10-minute segment, simply export two back-to-back 5-minute clips (e.g., 00:10:00–00:15:00 and 00:15:00–00:20:00).
  • Using private or age/member-gated URLs: Online cloud clippers can only process publicly accessible YouTube videos and archived public broadcasts.
