How to Cut a YouTube Video for PowerPoint & Google Slides (Offline MP4 & Timestamp Embeds)

Published by YTCuttr Team • Updated November 26, 2026 • 6 min read
Quick Answer Microsoft PowerPoint cannot trim an online YouTube URL directly—you must insert a trimmed MP4 file via Insert → Video → This Device (which also prevents mid-presentation Wi-Fi drops and YouTube ads). Google Slides, Google Classroom, and Notion support exact start/end timestamps natively or via the YTCuttr Timestamp Link Generator.

Nothing breaks a classroom lecture or client pitch faster than clicking play on an embedded YouTube video and getting hit with a 15-second unskippable ad, buffering hotel Wi-Fi, or accidentally playing the wrong two minutes of a documentary.

Depending on whether you present in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or a learning platform like Canvas/Google Classroom, here are the cleanest ways to show only the exact segment you need.

Method 1: PowerPoint (Offline MP4 Clip — Zero Ads, Zero Buffering)

If you paste an online YouTube URL into PowerPoint (Insert → Video → Online Videos), PowerPoint disables the Trim Video button and still requires live internet access. Cutting your 30-to-90-second segment first keeps your .pptx file small and completely immune to Wi-Fi issues:

  1. Open YTCuttr.com, paste the YouTube URL, set your exact Start and End timestamps, and download the segment in 1080p or 720p MP4 (720p keeps PowerPoint decks compact and emails easily).
  2. In Microsoft PowerPoint, go to Insert → Video → This Device and select your trimmed MP4 file.
  3. Select the video on your slide, open the Playback tab on the top ribbon, and set Start: Automatically or In Click Sequence. *(Need an extra half-second trim? Click Trim Video right inside the Playback tab.)*

Method 2: Google Slides (Built-in Start & End Timestamps)

If your school or office allows YouTube streaming and you prefer not to store MP4 files:

  1. In Google Slides, click Insert → Video, switch to the YouTube tab, paste the link, and click Insert.
  2. Click the video box, open Format options on the right panel, and expand Video playback.
  3. Enter your exact Start at and End at times (e.g., 02:15 to 03:05) so the slide automatically stops at your end timestamp.

Method 3: Google Classroom, Canvas, Notion & Blogs (No Download Needed)

YouTube’s normal Share link (?t=XX) only sets a start time—the video keeps playing right through the end unless you configure embed parameters manually. Frequently Asked Questions

Can Microsoft PowerPoint trim an embedded YouTube URL directly?

No. PowerPoint disables the Trim Video tool for online YouTube embeds. Cut the segment into an MP4 first on YTCuttr and insert it via Insert → Video → This Device.

How do I play only part of a YouTube video in Google Slides?

Insert the YouTube video, open Format options → Video playback, and type your Start at and End at timestamps.

Why do presenters prefer an offline MP4 clip over embedding a YouTube link?

Offline MP4 files never buffer on conference Wi-Fi, never get blocked by school firewalls, and never show surprise ads before your clip plays.
