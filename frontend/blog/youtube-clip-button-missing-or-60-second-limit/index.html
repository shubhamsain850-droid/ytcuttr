YouTube Clip Button Missing in 2026? Why It Disappeared & How to Cut Clips Longer Than 60s

Published by YTCuttr Team • Updated November 26, 2026 • 5 min read
Short Answer It isn’t a bug on your browser or account. YouTube officially retired viewer-created Clips in April 2026 and replaced the scissors button with Share at Timestamp. To create a bounded start-and-end segment link, use the YTCuttr Timestamp Link Generator; to save an actual MP4 clip (even longer than 60 seconds, up to 5 minutes), use YTCuttr.com.

If you scrolled below a YouTube video or live stream looking for the familiar scissors Clip icon and couldn’t find it, you aren’t alone. For years, viewer Clips allowed anyone to loop a 5-to-60-second snippet—though it never allowed downloading an MP4 file or cutting anything longer than one minute.

What Happened to the YouTube Clip Feature?

  • Viewer Clips Retired (April 2026): YouTube phased out viewer-created Clips in favor of an expanded Share at Timestamp menu on desktop and mobile. Existing old clips remain viewable, but no new viewer clips can be created natively.
  • Creators Still Have Studio Clips: If you own the channel, you can still cut segments from your own uploads inside YouTube Studio (using Video Clips or Remix into Shorts).
