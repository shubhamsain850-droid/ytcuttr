Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about cutting and downloading YouTube audio

How do I cut audio from a YouTube video?
Paste your YouTube link into YTCuttr. In Option 1 “Cut Audio (Timestamp)”, enter your exact Start Time and End Time. Then click “Cut & Download Audio”. Your trimmed audio file will be ready to download in seconds.
Can I download only a specific part of a YouTube video as audio?
Yes! That is exactly what YTCuttr is built for. You don’t need to download huge 1-hour or 2-hour video files just to get a 30-second music clip or speech soundbite. Simply set your timestamps and download only that exact audio portion.
What is the difference between Option 1 (Cut Audio) and Option 2 (Full Audio)?
Option 1 allows you to cut out any slice of the audio between two timestamps (e.g. from 01:15 to 02:30). Option 2 downloads the complete, uncut audio track from the entire video without needing to set any times.
What audio format is downloaded, and is it compatible with my music player?
YTCuttr extracts native M4A audio encoded in AAC. M4A offers higher audio quality and fidelity than standard MP3 while keeping file sizes small. It plays natively on all modern smartphones, tablets, Windows Media Player, QuickTime, iTunes, VLC, and in car stereos.
How can I use this to make a ringtone for my phone?
Set the start time to where your favorite chorus or music intro begins, and set the end time 30 to 45 seconds later. Download the cut audio file, and set it as your phone ringtone or alarm sound on Android or iOS.
Does this tool work on mobile phones without installing apps?
Yes, YTCuttr is fully web-based and optimized for mobile screens. You can use it directly in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android without downloading third-party apps or browser extensions.
Is YTCuttr free to use, and is there any audio watermark?
YTCuttr is 100% free with zero watermarks, audio tags, or hidden fees. All audio files downloaded are completely clean and unaltered.
