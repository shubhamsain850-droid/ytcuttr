What is the difference between WAV, FLAC, and MP3?
WAV is completely uncompressed studio audio, ideal for music production and video editing. FLAC is compressed lossless audio with identical quality but 50% smaller file size. MP3 is a compressed format designed for universal playback on all phones and car stereos.
